About the lab

PAIR stands for Providence AI and Robotics Lab. Our name reflects the collaboration between humans and machines that guides our work.

At PAIR Lab, we help students connect what they learn in the classroom to real problems. They investigate how intelligent systems sense the world, make decisions, and act, while considering who benefits from the technology and how it should be used.

Research

Our work brings together robotics, machine learning, and efficient computing, with applications in the environment, healthcare, and education.

01

Autonomous robots

We study how robots navigate, gather information, and make decisions in uncertain environments. Applications include environmental monitoring and collaboration among multiple robots.

02

AI for healthcare

We explore how machine learning and signal processing can help analyze health-related data, including movement signals used in tremor assessment.

03

Efficient computing

We investigate GPU-accelerated algorithms and parallel computing for problems such as estimating a system’s state from sensor measurements.

04

Physical AI Education

We investigate how students demonstrate their understanding of AI through experiments with physical systems.

Undergraduate research

Curiosity is a good place to start.

You don’t need prior experience in robotics or AI to get started. Bring an interest in learning, a willingness to investigate, and questions you want to explore.

What you can learn

  1. Develop your foundation

    Apply algorithms, data structures, and the basics of AI and machine learning.

  2. Build and investigate

    Learn a robotic platform, work with sensors and motors, and test your ideas through experiments.

  3. Share what you discover

    Discuss your progress in lab meetings and communicate your findings to the research community.
